Datasets and Project Goals

Introduction

Focusing on the 13th UN Sustainable Development Goal of Climate Action, our project explores data on the growing record of emissions and tracks patterns depicting climate change across each country.

Data Sources

Our project uses the datasets "Data on Energy by Our World in Data" and "CO2 and Greenhouse Gas Emissions by Our World in Data". Both were downloaded on 1/14/2026

Energy Dataset

  • URL: Energy-Data Github

  • Authors: Pablo Rosado, Hannah Ritchie, Edouard Mathieu, and Max Roser

CO2 Dataset

  • URL: CO2-Data Github

  • Authors: Pablo Rosado, Hannah Ritchie, Max Roser, Edouard Mathieu, and Bobbie Macdonald

What is in the Data?

From the energy and CO2 datasets, we inherited the columns:

  • country: Geographic location recorded
  • year: The year of observation
  • co2: Annual total emissions of carbon dioxide (CO₂), excluding land-use change (measured in million tonnes)
  • co2_per_capita: Carbon dioxide (CO₂) emissions from burning fossil fuels and industrial processes
  • energy_per_capita: Primary energy consumption per capita (measured in kilowatt-hours per person)
  • biofuel_electricity: Electricity generation from bioenergy (measured in terawatt-hours)
  • gas_electricity: Electricity generation from gas (measured in terawatt-hours)

We plan to use specific columns in the future, including:

  • ISO and population: integrate a map into our website.

Our Goal

The goal of our project is to make global emissions data accessible and interactive, allowing users to explore country-level trends through different contexts
